# Humans&

> Humans& (humansand.ai) is a human-centric frontier AI lab announced January 20, 2026, reimagining AI
> around people and their relationships. Research focuses on long-horizon and multi-agent reinforcement
> learning, memory, and user understanding. No public developer API, SDKs, or developer portal exist yet;
> this is a pre-product company profile in the API Evangelist network.
